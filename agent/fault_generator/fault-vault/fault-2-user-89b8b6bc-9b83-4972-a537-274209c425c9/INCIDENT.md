# Incident: Anonymous Users Sharing Shopping Carts

## Description
We are receiving reports from users that items they did not add are appearing in their shopping carts. Additionally, some users are reporting that their carts are being emptied unexpectedly or that they are seeing other people's items. This issue appears to affect only users who are not logged in (anonymous users). Metrics show a significant drop in the number of unique active carts and an unusually high number of items per cart for anonymous sessions.
